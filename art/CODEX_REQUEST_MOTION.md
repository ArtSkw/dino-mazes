# Codex art request: rigged "living" small assets

14 October 2026, Claude Code, after the owner approved the motion proposal
([../docs/MOTION_PROPOSAL.md](../docs/MOTION_PROPOSAL.md)). Execute with the built-in
**GPT Imagegen** skill. Machine-readable jobs: [prompts/jobs-motion.json](prompts/jobs-motion.json)
(58 jobs, generated by `python3 tools/art/make_motion_jobs.py`). Claude does all extraction,
rigging and animation in code afterwards.

## Why parts

Today every small asset is one flat picture moved as a rigid sheet (the butterfly "flaps" by being
squashed, treasures bob as stickers). Delivered in **parts**, the same painted art can move like a
cartoon: wings hinge, petals sway out of step, berries jiggle one by one, a highlight stays still
while a coconut rolls, a chest lid pops open. The look stays our painted style; the life comes
from code (springs, anticipation, overlapping action, follow-through).

## How to generate (the V3 method)

1. **Assembled master first**, one per asset, on a 1024 × 1024 transparent canvas, object centred
   at about 70 % of the canvas. References: `source/treasure-kit-v1.png`, `source/foliage-kit-v1.png`,
   `source/ui-surfaces-v1.png` (match the existing designs; the treasures must stay recognisably
   the same objects). Approve the masters before the parts.
2. **Each part = an edit of the master** that keeps the canvas, scale and position exactly and
   erases everything else. Parts must be **complete**: where another part covered them (wing
   roots under the body, petal bases under the centre, berries behind berries) the shape and
   painting continue a little under that area, so rotation never reveals a gap.
3. **Highlights and glints** (coconut, stone, shell) are their own translucent layers; the body
   part is repainted evenly lit where the highlight was.
4. Extra poses (perched butterfly, open chest, pressing hand) are edits of the master on the
   same canvas.

Never bake text, cast shadows between parts or floor shadows. True alpha only.

## Codex self-check

- [ ] Masters approved by the owner (treasures recognisably the same as today).
- [ ] Stacking all parts of an asset in order reproduces its master (no gaps, no doubled edges);
      check by compositing in a browser on cream and dark green.
- [ ] Every part on the master's canvas, same size and position; true alpha, no halos.
- [ ] Hidden overlaps painted (rotate a wing or petal by ±20° in the preview: no hole appears).
- [ ] Prompts and any revision prompts saved next to the jobs file.

## What Claude builds after delivery

- **Packing** (extend `tools/art/v3.py`): trim each part, record its offset in the master and a
  pivot (measured: wing roots, petal bases, stem foot, lid hinge), pack one atlas for all
  treasure, butterfly, chest and confetti parts (≈ 2–4 MiB of GPU memory).
- **Rig format**: a small JSON per asset (parts, draw order, pivots, rest pose) and one rig
  player for Phaser (containers) plus one for the DOM (collection screen, quest card), both driven
  by the same motion tokens and a `spring(t, ζ, f)` that is a pure function of time.
- **Motions** as described in the table and in MOTION_PROPOSAL.md §4, with reduced motion
  showing the master as a still image.
- **QA**: stills at named times, contact sheets and onion skins rendered by the Playwright tools,
  checked at real size on tablet and laptop, plus the GPU budget test extended to the new atlas.
