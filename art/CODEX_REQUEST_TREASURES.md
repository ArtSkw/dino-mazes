# Codex art request: four new treasures

> Status, 14 October 2026: **delivered and in the game** (art/CLAUDE_HANDOFF_TREASURES.md). The
> eighth classic treasure (strawberry) and Giganto's badge continue in
> [CODEX_REQUEST_FINISH.md](CODEX_REQUEST_FINISH.md).

14 October 2026, Claude Code, after the owner asked for ten treasures: **seven classic ones** found
with the friends and **three of Giganto's own**, found only on his quests. Execute with the built-in
**GPT Imagegen** skill. Machine-readable jobs: [prompts/jobs-treasures.json](prompts/jobs-treasures.json)
(26 jobs, generated by `python3 tools/art/make_treasure_jobs.py`).

The game is already built for them: each treasure switches on by itself once its approved image
exists and Claude runs the art export. Until then the game keeps the six treasures it has.

Each one must read as a **distinct silhouette at 80 px** (unfound treasures show as a flat
silhouette in the collection): the amber must not look like the round turquoise pebble, and the
giga flower must not look like the small coral hibiscus. Giganto's three should feel a little more
precious and glowing than the classic ones, never scary or sharp.

References: `source/treasure-kit-v1.png` (the six current treasures) and the approved motion
masters in `source/motion/*/master.png` (same painted style and canvas).

## How to generate (the same method as CODEX_REQUEST_MOTION.md)

1. **Master first**, one per treasure: transparent canvas, object centred at about 70 % of the
   canvas. If Imagegen fills more (the motion masters came back at about 90 %), **pad the canvas;
   never crop**. The owner approves the four masters before anything else.
2. After approval, copy each master unchanged to `source/treasures-v2/<id>.png`. That folder is the
   approval gate: Claude's export reads only from it (`tools/art/extract.py`).
3. **Parts** (priority 2, after approval) are edits of the master that keep canvas, scale and
   position and paint hidden overlaps, exactly as in the motion request. They let the treasures
   move like the others later (petals open, glints slide, the leaf in the amber sways).
4. Optional (priority 3): a round painted badge for the "Skarby Giganto" box in the collection,
   Giganto's three-toed footprint in golden sand. No text. The game uses a simple drawn footprint
   until then.

Never bake text, floor shadows or shadows between parts. True alpha only.

- [ ] Subject about 70 % of the canvas (padded, not cropped); true alpha, no halos, no shadows.
